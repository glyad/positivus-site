# Code of conduct

Contributors must keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and safe. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and give clear technical context. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct threatens the project or its community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the platform's available moderation channel.
